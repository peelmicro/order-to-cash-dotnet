An arming table produced without a forced rebuild proves nothing (`CLAUDE.md`, arming protocol).

> **A task whose own prose asserts a count, an identity, an ordering or an absence is a guard whether or not it is flagged — and in this list every such task IS flagged.** **39 of the 59 tasks below carry ⚑**, which is deliberately a lot: two features in a row shipped an unguarded countable claim because the claim sat in an unflagged task. If you find a countable claim below with no ⚑, arm it anyway and say so in the report.

> **Deleting the emission is one mutation family, not the whole of arming.** Every fact-emitting branch here gets **both** questions asked of it: does the guard fail when the row is absent (`F7`, `F8`, `F10`), and does it fail when a **field** is wrong (`F9`)?
